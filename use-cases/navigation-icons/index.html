Navigation Icons

Use familiar directional and wayfinding concepts without inventing your own icon vocabulary.

13 curated concepts · open source

Why this collection exists

Open-source navigation icons for menus, breadcrumbs, tabs, search, links and moving through digital products.

Practical guidance

  • Use directional icons when the action itself is spatial or sequential.
  • Pair icon-only navigation controls with an accessible name.
  • Keep the same directional meaning consistent across the product.

How should these icons be used?
