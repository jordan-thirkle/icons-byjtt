# JTT Icons — Art Direction v2

Status: **LOCKED — Art Direction v2 signed off for the 300-icon catalogue.** Catalogue expansion remains frozen.

## Canonical visual grammar

- 24×24 master coordinate system.
- 2px canonical stroke at 24px.
- round caps and joins for line icons.
- currentColor inheritance; no decorative fills.
- target optical live area: 18×18, but perceived weight takes precedence over mathematical bounds.
- compact silhouettes with deliberate negative space.
- controlled corner radii; avoid accidental radius mixing inside a family.
- orthogonal construction and clean 45° diagonals where the concept supports them.
- modifiers must remain legible at 12px before secondary detail is retained.
- parent/variant families share construction geometry, not merely semantic names.
- brand marks are treated as explicit exceptions and judged for recognisability rather than forced into generic line geometry.

## Five-size review

Every canonical icon is reviewed at:

12px — recognition, stroke survival, collapsed counters, tangencies.
14px — first practical UI size; weight and spacing must settle.
16px — primary interface size; silhouette and balance must be immediately legible.
20px — larger UI/control size; negative space and modifiers must remain intentional.
24px — canonical master; construction, radii, terminals and family relationships are judged here.

A technically valid SVG is not considered art-complete until all five sizes pass.

## Family review order

1. AI: agent, model, prompt, generate, chat, search, code.
2. Brands: recognisability and mark-specific optical exceptions.
3. People: user/profile/contact/contacts/team/users and state variants.
4. Files: folder/file/document/book/archive families and state/modifier variants.
5. Navigation: arrows, chevrons, corners, panels and transport controls.
6. Communication: mail/chat/comment/message/share/reaction families.
7. Media: play/pause/stop/volume/skip/repeat/shuffle families.
8. Developer: code/git/database/server/terminal/cloud-code families.
9. Commerce: cart/bag/payment/receipt/money/wallet families.
10. Objects: cloud/map/location/lock/key/globe/device families.
11. Actions: check/x/plus/minus/transfer/search/zoom/edit families.
12. System: status, warning, error, loading, connectivity and maintenance families.

## Failure classes

- optical weight mismatch
- accidental tangency
- collapsed negative space
- over-detailed micro form
- weak semantic silhouette
- inconsistent radius/terminal construction
- parent/variant drift
- visually near-identical unrelated concepts
- brand-mark distortion
- generic geometry that does not reinforce the JTT construction grammar

## Release rule

No catalogue expansion until the five-size review, family review, near-duplicate review and final JTT distinctiveness pass are explicitly signed off.


## Final 300-icon optical sign-off — 2026-09-28

The 300-icon catalogue has completed the release-gated optical pass.

- **300 canonical icons × 5 sizes = 1,500 rendered instances reviewed.**
- Review sizes: **12px, 14px, 16px, 20px, 24px**.
- All **11 catalogue categories** were reviewed family-by-family.
- The complete 300-icon catalogue masters were also reviewed at all five sizes.
- The optical renderer was corrected to preserve each canonical SVG root fill, stroke, stroke-width, caps, joins and other rendering attributes before the final review.
- **26 icons were redesigned** where the visual review exposed weak, generic, ambiguous or placeholder-level geometry.
- The redesigned icons were regenerated and visually re-reviewed across all five sizes.
- The final review confirms the catalogue follows the construction grammar above, with intentional exceptions only where brand recognisability or semantic convention requires them.
